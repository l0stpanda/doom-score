# Doom Map decisions

Decisions for the Doom Map (`map/`). Model decisions (D01–D110) are in `docs/KEY_DECISIONS.md`.
Numbers quoted here come from `map/data/derived.json` and `reports/map_derived/*.csv`.

| ID | Decision | Alternatives | Why | Needs review (Y/N) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M01 | Score and show **MLB and NHL only**. Leagues, seasons and teams are read from `doom_predictions.csv`, the location table and the logo manifest; no league list in code. | Six leagues (original spec) | Pivot spec §3. Work on NBA/NFL/WNBA/PWHL locations and research stopped at the audit; nothing from it was kept. | N |
| M02 | Map season *Y* = MLB season *Y* + NHL season *Y*–*Y+1* (both `season_start_year = Y`). Season control offers only seasons with predictions: 2008–2026 (MLB 2008–12 and 2026 alone). | Align by prediction date (MLB Nov *Y* with NHL Jun *Y*) | Same key as the data and the scatter; no reshuffling of seasons. | Y |
| M03 | A team-season's location = the city of the venue where it played most regular-season home games. Temporary homes count where played: Blue Jays 2020 Buffalo, Rays 2025 Tampa (Steinbrenner Field), Athletics 2025– West Sacramento. Split seasons use the plurality venue (Blue Jays 2021: 21 Dunedin / 23 Buffalo / rest Toronto → Toronto; Islanders 2018–20 Brooklyn/Uniondale split → Brooklyn, same metro). | Franchise's nominal home city | Pivot spec: show where the team actually played. Region averages therefore move with temporary homes (Blue Jays 2020 stays in the Golden Horseshoe anyway). | Y |
| M04 | Each location row carries `verified`: `official-city` (city on an official league/team page, 622 rows), `official-arena` (arena named on an official page, city from the arena's location, 317), `unverified` (35: Hurricanes/Raleigh and Kings/LA team pages did not load or name the arena; Coyotes/Glendale 2013–21). Relocation years confirmed on mlb.com / nhl.com: Coyotes → Utah approved Apr 18 2024, first season 2024-25; Coyotes at Mullett Arena (Tempe) 2022-23 and 2023-24; Athletics' last Oakland season 2024, Sacramento 2025–27. Thrashers → Jets (2011) predates the first NHL prediction season (2013), so all scored Jets seasons are in Winnipeg. | Fill from memory without a flag | Pivot spec §2.1: verify against official sources and say which rows could not be. | Y |
| M05 | Location table has the required columns plus `season_team_name`, `venue`, `verified`, `note`, `metro_id`. | Required columns only | The card needs the season's name ("Arizona Coyotes" in 2015, not "Utah Mammoth"); `verified` makes M04 auditable. | N |
| M06 | US geography: Census cartographic boundary counties 2023, 1:5M (Connecticut uses its 2022 planning regions). Contiguous US + DC; Alaska, Hawaii and territories are outside the study area. | 1:500k (larger), states | Recognisable county lines, small file. | N |
| M07 | Canadian geography: StatCan 2021 census divisions (cartographic) via StatCan's own ArcGIS REST service at 0.005° generalisation. The zip download returns HTTP 403 to scripts. Provinces only; territories are outside the study area. | geoBoundaries ADM2 (only 76 economic regions, no province field) | Same official source as the zip; all 293 divisions. | N |
| M08 | **Region assignment:** each county / census division goes to the nearest *fan-identity anchor* (great-circle from its interior point), choosing only among the regions its state/province allows (`map/data_src/region_rules.csv`, `region_anchors.csv`, 226 anchors; `region_overrides.csv` is empty). Regions are geography only, fixed across seasons; all 23 original regions are kept, including ones with no MLB/NHL team in some seasons. Judgment calls: Windsor/Chatham-Kent → Great Lakes; Kenora/Rainy River → Prairies; Kingston and Thunder Bay → Golden Horseshoe; Fairfield/New Haven/Naugatuck CT → New York; Mercer NJ → Philadelphia; Syracuse → New York, Rochester → Golden Horseshoe; Centre and Erie PA → Ohio Valley; Austin → South Texas; El Paso → Desert Southwest; Fresno → NorCal, Kern → SoCal; Iowa split three ways; central Nevada → Desert SW (Tonopah anchor). | Census divisions / whole states; Voronoi of teams | Fan identity rather than census lines (original spec §3), using recognisable borders. A rule plus data is reviewable and editable. | Y |
| M09 | Atlantic Canada, rest of Québec → Ottawa–Montréal; northern Ontario → Golden Horseshoe (NW Ontario → Prairies). | New team-less regions | Keeps the 23 regions; every piece of land in one region. | Y |
| M10 | The Census and StatCan borders leave hairline gaps of ≤ ~300 m along the US–Canada border (measured 20–290 m). They are kept and hidden at render time with a same-colour stroke. These gaps are < 1 px at every zoom. | Snap vertices ≥ 0.001° | Snapping at 0.001–0.005° closed the St. Clair / Niagara rivers and turned Lake Erie into land. | Y |
| M11 | Manassas and Manassas Park (VA) collapse at 1:5M; their land is absorbed by Prince William County (DMV). | — | Artefact of the generalised file; region unchanged. | N |
| M12 | Context land (not part of any region): Natural Earth 50m via `world-atlas` for Mexico/Caribbean, Alaska and the territories only, so the Great Lakes show as water. | No context; full US/Canada context | Kept with the user's approval at the audit. | N |
| M13 | Study crop 24–57°N, 52–128°W, for display and the land grid. | Whole provinces | Spec: crop the empty north. Fort McMurray (56.7°N) is the northernmost anchor. | N |
| M14 | **Too new to score** = a located team-season with no row in `doom_predictions.csv`. Today this is **0**: Vegas (2017) and Seattle (2021) have predictions from their first season. | Rule on seasons played | Pivot spec §3: from the data, not assumed. | N |
| M15 | Logos: only `team_meta.csv` URLs, cached git-ignored by `dashboard/fetch_logos.py`. An identity uses the logo only if the season name still contains today's nickname; otherwise a monogram (PHX/ARI Coyotes, Utah Hockey Club, CLE Indians). Monogram = rule + 17 official-abbreviation overrides (`data_src/monogram_overrides.csv`), unique within each league. | Today's logo for every season | A Mammoth logo in Glendale would be wrong about *who*. | N |
| M16 | **Standardisation:** z = (doom − mean) / SD within each league-season (population SD), for `doom_pred`. `actual_doom` is standardised the same way for the actual view. Every colour, average, rank and list uses z; cards also show raw doom and band. | Raw doom; percentile ranks | Pivot spec §5. | N |
| M17 | **Caveat (for the write-up):** standardising makes MLB look as certain as NHL. MLB's final model is standings only, so MLB z is a rescaled standings rank (bounded ±1.68). Measured stretch is small: within-season SD 11.6 (MLB) vs 12.5 (NHL) doom points. The bigger issue is accuracy: MLB rank correlation 0.41 vs NHL 0.61, ±30-point intervals. | — | Pivot spec §5. | Y |
| M18 | **σ = 100 mi**, by rule: the largest σ in 50–400 (25-mi steps) with median own-metro share ≥ 0.6, ≤ 5% of land > 300 mi from any team at visible weight (W ≥ 0.1), and every close pair (Baltimore–DC, LA–Anaheim, Toronto–Buffalo) blending more than the far pair (Dallas–Houston). σ 125 fails only the last test (Houston ← Dallas 0.27 > DC ← Baltimore 0.15). | 75 (blobby, separate cities); 125 (smoother, own-metro median 0.615) | Pivot spec §6; diagnostics in `reports/map_derived/sigma_diagnostics.csv`. | Y |
| M19 | **Opacity:** linear in total weight W, 0 at W ≤ 0.0122 (95th percentile of W on land > 300 mi from any team) and full at W ≥ 1.0703 (10th percentile of W at metro centres ≈ one team's full weight). Mixed in OKLab over the "no teams" grey. **σ and both cutoffs must be re-derived when leagues are added**; `derive.py` does it automatically. | Smoothstep; hand-set cutoffs | Pivot spec §6. | Y |
| M20 | **Colour scale:** one fixed scale, ends at z −1.677 / +1.678 (2nd / 98th percentile of all 974 predicted z), clamped beyond them (19 below, 20 above). Gold sits at z = 0. Actual z uses the same scale (57 of 788 outside). | Symmetric ±2; per-season | Pivot spec §7. | N |
| M21 | Default palette blue `#1F5FD6` / gold `#F2B400` / crimson `#C21F32` / grey `#A3A8B3` passes every spec check (`reports/map_palette.png`); no hex changed. Dark-mode contrast: blue 3.14, crimson 3.02 (narrow pass), grey 7.54. Green–red toggle fails deuteranopia ends (ΔE 8.9 < 20), as expected. | — | Pivot spec §7. | N |
| M22 | **Open:** the OKLab blue → gold half crosses neutral grey. The ramp at t = 0.25 (z ≈ −0.84) is `#8C949C`, ΔE2000 6.1–6.9 from the "no teams" grey in all four vision types. In test maps, moderately good areas read as "no teams", and the bright gold midpoint shows as bands where good and bad teams meet (Calgary–Edmonton). Measured options: (C) lighter grey `#C4C7CE` → closest ΔE ≥ 13.8 everywhere, dull zone remains; (B) chroma held → hard blue/gold jump at z ≈ −0.75; (F) OKLCh via green → fails protanopia (ΔE 3.0). Palette left as specified pending your call. | See left | Colour must never imply "no data" where there are teams (honesty rule). | **Y** |
| M23 | Tooling: geometry with mapshaper (Node), tables and checks in Python, check renders with Playwright driving the installed Edge. One command: `python map/build_map.py`. Generated data in `map/data/` is committed; `node_modules/` and `map/check/` are ignored. | geopandas/shapely | Topology-aware dissolve and simplify, and the browser map is JS anyway. | N |
