# Doom Map decisions

Decisions for the Doom Map (`map/`). Model decisions (D01–D110) are in `docs/KEY_DECISIONS.md`.
Numbers quoted here come from `map/data/derived.json` and `reports/map_derived/*.csv`.

| ID | Decision | Alternatives | Why | Needs review (Y/N) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M01 | Score and show **MLB and NHL only**. Leagues, seasons and teams are read from `doom_predictions.csv`, the location table and the logo manifest; no league list in code. | Six leagues (original spec) | Pivot spec §3. Work on NBA/NFL/WNBA/PWHL locations and research stopped at the audit; nothing from it was kept. | N |
| M02 | Map season *Y* = MLB season *Y* + NHL season *Y*–*Y+1* (both `season_start_year = Y`). | M03 | A team-season's location = the city of the venue where it played most regular-season home games. Thrashers → Jets (2011) predates the first NHL prediction season (2013), so all scored Jets seasons are in Winnipeg. | Fill from memory without a flag | Pivot spec §2.1: verify against official sources and say which rows could not be. | Y |

The M08 calls were accepted at the review. | New team-less regions | Keeps the 23 regions; every piece of land in one region. | N |

| M15 | Logos: only `team_meta.csv` URLs, cached git-ignored by `dashboard/fetch_logos.py`. Monogram = rule + 17 official-abbreviation overrides (`data_src/monogram_overrides.csv`), unique within each league. | Today's logo for every season | A Mammoth logo in Glendale would be wrong about *who*. | N |
| M16 | **Standardisation:** z = (doom − mean) / SD within each league-season (population SD), for `doom_pred`. `actual_doom` is standardised the same way for the actual view. Every colour, average, rank and list uses z; cards also show raw doom and band. | Raw doom; percentile ranks | Pivot spec §5. | N |
| M17 | **Caveat (for the write-up):** standardising makes MLB look as certain as NHL. MLB's final model is standings only, so MLB z is a rescaled standings rank (bounded ±1.68). Measured stretch is small: within-season SD 11.6 (MLB) vs 12.5 (NHL) doom points. The bigger issue is accuracy: MLB rank correlation 0.41 vs NHL 0.61, ±30-point intervals. | — | Pivot spec §5. | Y |
| M18 | **σ = 100 mi**, by rule: the largest σ in 50–400 (25-mi steps) with median own-metro share ≥ 0.6, ≤ 5% of land > 300 mi from any team at visible weight (W ≥ 0.1), and every close pair (Baltimore–DC, LA–Anaheim, Toronto–Buffalo) blending more than the far pair (Dallas–Houston). σ 125 fails only the last test (Houston ← Dallas 0.27 > DC ← Baltimore 0.15). **Final** (Phase-1 review). | 75 (blobby, separate cities); 125 (smoother, own-metro median 0.615) | Pivot spec §6; diagnostics in `reports/map_derived/sigma_diagnostics.csv`. | N |
| M19 | **Opacity:** a function of total weight W (smootherstep since M26; was linear), 0 at W ≤ 0.0122 (95th percentile of W on land > 300 mi from any team) and full at W ≥ 1.0703 (10th percentile of W at metro centres ≈ one team's full weight). Mixed in OKLab over the "no teams" grey. **σ and both cutoffs must be re-derived when leagues are added**; `derive.py` does it automatically. | Smoothstep; hand-set cutoffs | Pivot spec §6. | Y |
| M20 | **Colour scale:** one fixed scale, ends at z −1.677 / +1.678 (2nd / 98th percentile of all 974 predicted z), clamped beyond them (19 below, 20 above). Gold sits at z = 0. Actual z uses the same scale (57 of 788 outside). | Symmetric ±2; per-season | Pivot spec §7. | N |
| M21 | Default palette stops blue `#1F5FD6` / gold `#F2B400` / crimson `#C21F32` / grey `#A3A8B3` pass every end-point check (`reports/map_palette.png`); no stop hex changed. Green–red toggle fails deuteranopia ends (ΔE 8.9 < 20), as expected. | — | Pivot spec §7. | N |
| M22 | **Resolved by M24.** Phase 1 finding: the OKLab blue → gold half crosses neutral grey. The ramp at t = 0.25 (z ≈ −0.84) is `#8C949C`, ΔE2000 6.1–6.9 from the "no teams" grey in all four vision types. In test maps, moderately good areas read as "no teams", and the bright gold midpoint shows as bands where good and bad teams meet (Calgary–Edmonton). Measured options: (C) lighter grey `#C4C7CE` → closest ΔE ≥ 13.8 everywhere, dull zone remains; (B) chroma held → hard blue/gold jump at z ≈ −0.75; (F) OKLCh via green → fails protanopia (ΔE 3.0). | See left | Colour must never imply "no data" where there are teams (honesty rule). | N |
| M23 | Tooling: geometry with mapshaper (Node), tables and checks in Python, check renders with Playwright driving the installed Edge. One command: `python map/build_map.py`. Generated data in `map/data/` is committed; `node_modules/` and `map/check/` are ignored. | geopandas/shapely | Topology-aware dissolve and simplify, and the browser map is JS anyway. | N |
| M24 | **Ramp interpolation (Phase-1 review):** OKLCh, hue decreasing blue → cyan → green → gold → orange → crimson, gamut-mapped by lowering chroma at constant L and hue. New whole-ramp check: all 101 samples ≥ ΔE2000 15 from the grey for normal, deutan, protan and tritan vision; minimum chroma reported. Plain OKLCh fails (worst 6.6), and a chroma floor changes nothing (worst 6.6, chroma already above every floor). The best single teal stop reaches 9.7 (`palette_search.csv`, 1,354 candidates). The binding case is tritanopia near yellow-green (the tritan neutral, ~570 nm) at the grey's lightness. Chosen, the smoothest-L* passing variant: OKLCh with a **teal stop** (OKLCh 0.52 / 0.14 / 175° at t = 0.20) **and a darker yellow-green stop** (0.58 / 0.13 / 120° at t = 0.35). It crosses yellow-green below the grey's lightness. Whole-ramp minimum ΔE: normal 27.8, deutan 22.9, protan 19.0, tritan 15.4 (thin margin); min C*ab 27.4; max \|Δ²L*\| 2.33 (plain 1.21). Cost: a short olive stretch around t ≈ 0.35. The map uses 256-step lookup tables from `palette.json`. The green–red toggle (plain OKLCh) fails deutan ends (8.9) and the tritan whole-ramp check (9.0); reported, still the toggle. | OKLab (fails); OKLCh plain / + chroma floor / + teal only (all fail tritan or protan) | Review change 1: pick the option that passes all checks with the smoothest L*. The second stop goes beyond the families named; without it nothing passes. | Y |
| M25 | **Default season** = latest season with predictions in every league: **2025** (MLB 2025 + NHL 2025–26), from `derived.json`. A season missing some selected leagues shows a banner naming the leagues present ("MLB only this season"). | Latest season of any league (2026, MLB only) | Review change 4. | N |
| M26 | **Seams at render time:** (a) the field S = Σwz and W = Σw is computed on a 3-px grid, then both are blurred (Gaussian, 2.5 cells) before taking S/W, so it stays a weighted average; then bilinear upsampling and 4×4 ordered dither against 8-bit banding. (b) Opacity uses smootherstep instead of a clamped linear ramp: the clamp drew a visible ellipse (Mach band) at W = w1. Cutoffs w0/w1 unchanged. (c) The land mask is filled and stroked in one colour, so border hairlines (M10) never show. The gold band where good and bad teams meet is the z = 0 crossing, not a seam, and stays. | Bigger blur only (doesn't remove the clamp edge) | Review change 2: σ = 100 final, smooth seams at render time. | N |
| M27 | **Region ranking: empirical-Bayes shrinkage** of the region average of team z toward the mean of all scored teams in view: shrunk = μ + B(x̄ − μ), B = τ² / (τ² + s²/n). Method-of-moments over all 19 seasons: s² (within-region) = 1.00, τ² (between regions) = **0.030**. τ² is 0 in 11 of 19 seasons: regions barely differ beyond chance. So B = 0.03 (n = 1) to 0.13 (n = 5): rankings are heavily shrunk. Cards show the raw average and n; the ranked list draws the shrunk value as a bar and the raw average as a marker. Constants are fixed across league/season filters (`derived.json`); μ follows the filter. | Raw means (small regions swing to extremes); per-season τ² (often 0, so all ties) | Review change 5; honest about how little the regional signal is. | Y |
