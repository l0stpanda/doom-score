| M27 | **Region ranking: empirical-Bayes shrinkage** of the region average of team z toward the mean of all scored teams in view: shrunk = μ + B(x̄ − μ), B = τ² / (τ² + s²/n). Method-of-moments over all 19 seasons: s² (within-region) = 1.00, τ² (between regions) = **0.030**. τ² is 0 in 11 of 19 seasons: regions barely differ beyond chance. So B = 0.03 (n = 1) to 0.13 (n = 5): rankings are heavily shrunk. Cards show the raw average and n; the ranked list draws the shrunk value as a bar and the raw average as a marker. Constants are fixed across league/season filters (`derived.json`); μ follows the filter. | Raw means (small regions swing to extremes); per-season τ² (often 0, so all ties) | Review change 5; honest about how little the regional signal is. *Final review: approved; the finding is captioned in the list (M29) and the README.* | N |
